# Design System — Balance+ Alt-Design ("Precisión Clínica")

> Generated by `/design-consultation`. Scope: `web-alt/` only (see
> `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-18-web-alt-redesign-design.md`). Does not
> apply to `web/` (the original site, untouched).

## Product Context
- **What this is:** A medically-supervised GLP-1 obesity-treatment telemedicine
  program (marketing site + patient portal).
- **Who it's for:** Adults in Chile with overweight/obesity seeking supervised
  medical treatment — not casual dieters browsing a wellness app.
- **Space/industry:** GLP-1 / weight-loss telemedicine (direct-to-consumer
  health).
- **Project type:** Marketing/landing site with an embedded interactive
  calculator, plus authenticated patient portal (portal.html, intake.html —
  Phase 2, not built yet).
- **The one thing to remember:** "Confianza médica seria" — this is a real
  medical program, not another diet app.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Direction:** Precisión Clínica (Clinical Precision) — editorial/premium,
  closer to a serious medical publication than a DTC weight-loss funnel.
- **Decoration level:** Minimal — typography and whitespace carry the design;
  no illustration, no decorative shapes.
- **Mood:** Calm, credible, unhurried. The opposite of "urgent sale" energy.
- **Reference sites:** `https://glp1.medvi.org/` (primary competitive
  reference, screenshotted during research) — a direct GLP-1 telemedicine
  competitor using rounded sans typography (Red Hat Text), a warm cream/peach
  background, sage-green accents, UGC video testimonials, and direct-response
  marketing tactics (sale banners, urgency copy). Balance+ deliberately departs
  from this category convention — see Decisions Log.

## Typography
- **Display/Hero:** Fraunces (weight 500, optical size 9..144) — a serif with
  editorial authority; signals "medical publication," not "friendly app."
- **Body:** Instrument Sans (400/500/600) — precise, legible grotesk; avoids
  the Inter/system-ui "gave up on typography" default.
- **UI/Labels:** Instrument Sans 600, uppercase, 12px, letter-spacing 0.06–0.12em.
- **Data/Tables:** Geist (600, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) — used
  specifically for the BMI calculator's numeric readouts so digits don't
  jitter as they update.
- **Loading:** Google Fonts —
  `family=Fraunces:ital,opsz,wght@0,9..144,400;0,9..144,500;0,9..144,600;1,9..144,500&family=Instrument+Sans:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;0,600;1,400&family=Geist:wght@400;500;600`
- **Scale:** Hero H1 44px/1.1, section H2 30px, body 15–17px, small/eyebrow 12–13px.

## Color
- **Approach:** Restrained — one primary accent (forest), one sparing
  secondary accent (terracotta) reserved for human/testimonial moments only.
- **Ink (text):** `#1a1f2e` — near-black navy-charcoal, not pure black.
- **Forest (primary accent):** `#2f4a3d` — desaturated deep forest green;
  darker and more serious than the original `#8AB090` sage. Used for CTAs,
  tags, links, and the calculator result highlight.
- **Forest tint (accent surface):** `#e4ebe5`.
- **Terracotta (secondary accent):** `#b5603f` — warm, used only in
  testimonial/human-story sections, never in navigation or clinical data.
- **Terracotta tint:** `#f3e3da`.
- **Paper (background):** `#f7f4ef` — warm off-white, deliberately not stark
  white and not the peachy-cream Medvi uses.
- **Paper raised (cards):** `#fffdf9`.
- **Border:** `#e2ddd0`.
- **Muted (secondary text):** `#6b6459`.
- **Semantic:** success `#2f6b4f`, warning `#a1701f`, error `#a13f34`, info `#2f4a6b`.
- **Dark mode:** Full surface redesign, not just inverted — background
  `#14181f`, raised surface `#1c212b`, ink becomes `#f2efe8`, forest lifts to
  `#7ea88f` for contrast, terracotta lifts to `#d99674`. Implemented via
  `:root[data-theme="dark"]` CSS custom property overrides (see
  `/tmp/design-research/balance-plus-design-preview.html` for the reference
  implementation — copy the `:root` variable blocks directly).

## Spacing
- **Base unit:** 4px (with an 8px working rhythm for most components).
- **Density:** Spacious/editorial — more breathing room than the category
  standard (Medvi and peers run tighter, conversion-optimized spacing).
- **Scale:** 2xs(2) xs(4) sm(8) md(16) lg(24) xl(32) 2xl(48) 3xl(64).

## Layout
- **Approach:** Grid-disciplined, with one deliberate asymmetric editorial
  moment in the hero (62/38 split between headline copy and the calculator
  card, not centered).
- **Grid:** 12-column on desktop, single column under 860px.
- **Max content width:** 1180px.
- **Border radius:** sm 4px (buttons, inputs), md 8px (cards), lg 14px
  (hero container), full 999px (pill tags only — not buttons).

## Motion
- **Approach:** Minimal-functional — only transitions that aid comprehension
  (theme toggle fade, hover states). No scroll-driven choreography, no
  "urgency" animation (pulsing badges, countdown tickers, etc.) — a
  deliberate departure from the category's direct-response playbook.
- **Easing:** enter `ease-out`, exit `ease-in`, move `ease-in-out`.
- **Duration:** micro 100ms, short 200ms, medium 300ms.

## Safe Choices (kept from category convention)
- Real photography of people (not generic stock or illustration) — but styled
  documentary/editorial rather than bright DTC-smiling, to match the
  Clinical Precision mood.
- A prominent eligibility-first CTA ("¿Califico?" / "Evaluar mi elegibilidad")
  — the category convention users expect from a telemedicine intake flow.
- Mobile-first responsive layout.

## Deliberate Risks (where Balance+ breaks from the category)
1. **Serif display type instead of rounded friendly sans.** Every reference
   competitor (Medvi, and the broader Ro/Hims/Noom category) uses a rounded
   geometric sans. Fraunces signals editorial/clinical authority instead.
   Cost: slightly less "playful warmth" — offset by the terracotta accent in
   human-story moments.
2. **Desaturated, muted palette instead of bright cream + sage.** Reads
   premium/credible rather than "wellness app." Cost: risks feeling cold if
   the terracotta accent isn't used deliberately — hence its scoping rule
   (testimonials only).
3. **No urgency/sale marketing tactics** (no sale banners, no countdown
   timers, no "X sold today" social-proof tickers — all present on Medvi).
   Reinforces "this is a medical program," not a DTC offer. Deliberate
   trade-off against short-term conversion urgency, in service of the
   "confianza médica seria" positioning the user chose as the one thing to
   remember.
